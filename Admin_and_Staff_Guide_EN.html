2 Step-by-Step: Free Hosting on Cloudflare Pages (One-Time, 5 Mins)

Uploading the files inside deploy/ to Cloudflare Pages gives your church a permanent free link such as https://christumc-bulletin.pages.dev (or a custom subdomain like bulletin.christumc.cc).

  1. Go to cloudflare.com, create a free account, and verify your email.
  2. In the left sidebar, click Workers & Pages → Create application → Pages → Upload assets (Drag and drop your files).
  3. Enter a short project name (lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only, e.g., christumc-bulletin) and click Create project.
  4. Open your deploy/ folder on your computer, select all 7 files inside deploy/ (so index.html is at the top level—not nested inside an extra subfolder), and drag them into the upload box.
  5. Click Deploy site. Within seconds, your live URL (e.g., https://christumc-bulletin.pages.dev) is ready!

3 First-Time Church Setup (10 Mins)

  1. Open your live bulletin URL on a computer browser and click Admin in the top-right corner.
  2. Enter the initial password: admin1234. All editing boxes will appear across every tab.
  3. Scroll down on the Home tab to [Setup] Church Profile & Links:
    • Verify Church Name (Christ United Methodist Church), Motto (Gather · Grow · Go), and Website (https://www.christumc.cc).
    • Paste your direct Tithe.ly Online Giving URL into Online Giving URL.
    • Paste your YouTube or Facebook Livestream URL (if applicable) and optional CCLI License #.
    • Change the Admin Password from admin1234 to a new staff password.
  4. Optional: Under Church Logo & Sermon Graphic, upload your official high-resolution PNG logo if desired (a crisp Cross & Flame Christ UMC logo is already built in!).
  5. Scroll to the bottom of Home and click Download Deployment File (index.html).
  6. Replace deploy/index.html on your computer with the newly downloaded index.html, then go to Cloudflare Pages → your project → Create new deployment and drag all files inside deploy/ to publish!
Important: Edits made in Admin Mode stay only in your computer's browser until you click Download Deployment File (index.html) and upload the updated folder to Cloudflare Pages!

4 Weekly 10-Minute Routine (Every Friday/Saturday)

  1. Open your bulletin site on your computer, click Admin, and sign in.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of Home and click Start New Bulletin for Next Week.
    • This safely keeps your past bulletins in the Archive, advances the Sunday date by 7 days, keeps your Order of Worship structure, and clears last week's Scripture/Sermon boxes.
  3. Update each tab:
    • Home: Update song titles, Scripture reference, sermon title, and worship leaders in Order of Worship. Optionally upload the weekly Sermon Series graphic.
    • Sermon: Enter Sermon Title, Preacher, Scripture Reference & Verses, Sermon Notes, and Reflection Questions.
    • Hymns: Paste song/hymn lyrics (or upload sheet music / slide images).
    • News: Paste this week's church announcements.
  4. Return to the bottom of Home and click Download Deployment File (index.html).
  5. Move the downloaded index.html into your deploy/ folder (replacing last week's index.html), then upload all files inside deploy/ via Create new deployment on Cloudflare Pages.

5 Quick Formatting Cheat Sheet

A. Order of Worship (Home Tab)

Use * for each worship item, | for worship leaders (separate multiple service leaders with ;), † for standing items, and **asterisks** for bold congregational responses:

†Please stand as you are able

* Prelude — "Lord, I Lift Your Name on High" | Josh Hayag, Praise Team (8:00, 10:00 DR); Risë' Barker (10:00 YR)
* †Call to Worship
  When the world is loud and our hearts are restless, God calls us to be still and come near.
  **We come as God's children, and the door is open to us.**
* †Scripture — Matthew 6:6–10 | Kristin Rindt/Hannah (8:00); Mike Warren (10:00 DR)
* Sermon — "Teach Us How to Pray" | Rev. TK Lee

_New visitors are invited to see an usher after the service. We have a gift for you!_

B. Hymns & Worship Lyrics (Hymns Tab)

Start each song or hymn with # Song Title | Hymnal # or Performer, followed by the lyrics:

# †Doxology (UMH #95) | Praise God, from Whom All Blessings Flow
Praise God, from whom all blessings flow;
Praise him, all creatures here below;
Praise him above, ye heavenly host;
Praise Father, Son, and Holy Ghost. Amen.

C. Announcements (News Tab)

Separate announcements with a blank line. Start titles with 1. , date/location lines with : , and bullet points with - . Phone numbers, emails, and links automatically become tappable!

1. Senior Day at Christ UMC
: Wednesday, December 16 from 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM · Dining Room
Join every month for activities, lunch, and fellowship!
- Please RSVP with the office (info@christumc.cc / 815.399.5910) by Monday, December 7.

6 Connecting Google Sheets (Connect Cards, Prayers & RSVPs)

Connect Cards, Prayer Requests (Joys & Concerns), and Event RSVPs can automatically populate a private Google Spreadsheet in your church Google account.

  1. Sign in to Google Drive / Google Sheets (sheets.google.com) with a church Gmail account and create a Blank Spreadsheet named Christ UMC Bulletin Responses.
  2. In the top menu, click Extensions → Apps Script.
  3. Delete any placeholder code in Code.gs, open GoogleSheets_Code.gs from your bulletin folder in Notepad/TextEdit, copy the entire file, paste it into Apps Script, and click Save (disk icon).
  4. Click the blue Deploy button (top right) → New deployment.
  5. Click the gear icon next to "Select type" → choose Web app.
  6. Set these two fields carefully:
    • Execute as: Me
    • Who has access: Anyone (Required so congregation members don't have to sign in to submit a Connect Card or Prayer Request)
  7. Click Deploy → Authorize access → choose your Google account. If Google shows an "unverified app" warning, click Advanced → Go to project (unsafe) → Allow.
  8. Copy the generated Web app URL (make sure it ends with /exec, not /dev).
  9. Open your bulletin in Admin mode, paste the /exec URL into Google Sheets Web App URL on the Home tab, click Download Deployment File (index.html), and upload the updated deploy/ folder to Cloudflare Pages!
Automatic Tabs: You do not need to create columns manually! As members use the app, tabs named 1_VisitorLog, 2_ConnectCards, 3_PrayerRequests, and 4_CareAndRSVP are created automatically. Keep this Google Sheet private to authorized pastors and staff.

7 Sharing with the Congregation & Troubleshooting

  • QR Code at Doors & Slides: Generate a free QR code pointing to your bulletin URL and print it on welcome table signs or display it on pre-service slides.
  • Add to Phone Home Screen (Like an App):
    • iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share button (square with up arrow) → Add to Home Screen.
    • Android (Chrome): Tap the top-right Menu (⋮) → Add to Home screen / Install app.
  • Phone stuck on last week's bulletin? Close and reopen the app, or visit https://your-site.pages.dev/recovery.html to refresh the cache while keeping saved personal notes.
  • Forgot the Admin Password? Open deploy/index.html in Notepad/TextEdit and search for "adminPassword" to view it.
